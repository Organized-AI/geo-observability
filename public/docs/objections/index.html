    Brand perception

    Objections

    Objections are the drawbacks, caveats and warnings assistants attach to your brand: "more expensive than alternatives", "no international shipping", "mixed reviews on customer support".

    #Where they come from

    The same judge that finds attributes also pulls out objections from answers that name you. They're grouped and counted by how many answers raised them.

    #What to do with them

    For each common objection, ask three questions:

    1. Is it true? If not, it's probably coming from an old or wrong source. Check fact-checking and the sources cited in those chats, and consider a fix fact action.
    2. Is it true but out of date? Prices change, features ship. A page that clearly states the current position gives assistants something newer to cite. That's a refresh page action.
    3. Is it true and current? Then it's real feedback. Answering it honestly on your site ("Why we cost more") often works better than hoping it goes away.

    #Limits

    Objections are a model's summary of the answer text. Read the underlying chats before acting on one, especially if it rests on only a handful of answers.
