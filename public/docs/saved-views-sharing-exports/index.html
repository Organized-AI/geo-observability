    Saved views, sharing and exports

    #Saved views

    A saved view stores your filters (date range, engines, countries, topics, tags) and your widget layout. Save one for each recurring question, such as "Espresso, UK, last 30 days" or "Non-branded prompts only".

    Views belong to the project, so everyone on it can open them.

    #Share links

    From a saved view you can create a share link. Anyone with the link can see that view's overview without logging in.

    • Links are signed and read-only. Nobody can change anything through them.
    • Links expire, after 30 days by default. Choose a shorter time for sensitive data.
    • A link shows the project name and domain along with the numbers. Don't share it anywhere you wouldn't share a screenshot.

    To keep sharing after a link expires, create a new one.

    #CSV exports

    Two exports follow your current filters:

    • Chats CSV: one row per answer, with the details you see in the Chats list.
    • Metrics CSV: the headline metrics for your filter.

    Use them for your own analysis or to feed a client report.

    #PDF

    There's no separate PDF export. Open the Overview, set your filters, and print it from your browser with "Save as PDF" as the destination.

    If you'd rather pull numbers into another tool automatically, use the API instead of exports.
