How it works

From the question a buyer asks to the revenue it brings in

  1. 01

    Track the prompts that matter

    Start from your domain. We draft your profile, suggest topics and generate prompts with the mix of branded and unbranded, informational and buying-intent questions you choose. Each prompt runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, per country, on a daily or weekly schedule.

  2. 02

    Score every answer

    Every answer is read for which brands it names, in what order and in what tone, and which pages it cites. You get visibility, share of voice, average position, sentiment, win rate and cited rate, for you and every competitor, by engine, country, topic and tag.

  3. 03

    Tie it to your website

    A lightweight tap on your site and an optional edge worker log AI crawler hits and AI referral sessions, and your server-side tagging sends conversions. Each page is joined to the prompts that cited it, so you can see which answers drive real visits and revenue.

Visibility

The share of answers that mention you at all.

Share of voice

Position

Your average rank when you're named. Lower is better.

Sentiment

How favourably you're described, on a 0 to 100 scale.

Win rate

How often you're named ahead of the competitors you track.

Cited rate

How often an answer links to a page on your own domain.

The observability difference

Being mentioned is the start. Knowing what it's worth is the point.

Answer tracking tells you what assistants say. Observability tells you what happens next: which bots read your pages, which answers send people to you, and which of those visits convert.

  1. Prompt

    A buyer asks an assistant a question you track.

  2. Answer

    The answer names you, ranks you and cites a page.

  3. Crawler hits

    Training, search and retrieval bots that read that page, logged at the edge.

  4. Referral sessions

    Visits that arrive from an assistant, by assistant.

  5. Conversions

    Server-side events with value, matched to the landing page.

Answer tracking alone

  • Shows whether you were mentioned
  • Lists the sources assistants cite
  • Leaves you guessing which pages matter

With GEO Observability

  • One table per page: bot hits by type, citations, citation rate, sessions, conversions and revenue
  • The prompts that cited each page, one click away
  • Source gaps: sites that cite competitors but never you
  • Prioritised actions and content briefs drawn from the evidence
