## Top bar

- **Workspace switcher.** Shown if you belong to more than one workspace. Agency staff see every client here.
- **Project switcher.** Pick the brand you're looking at.
- **Filter bar.** Date range, engine, country, topic and tag. Filters carry across the report pages.
