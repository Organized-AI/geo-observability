Cited rate tells you how often assistants point readers to your own website.

## Definition

**Cited rate = answers that cite at least one of your domains ÷ answers in the filter.**

"Cited" means the engine showed the link to the reader as a source. Your domains are the ones listed on your own brand in [Competitors and aliases](/docs/competitors-and-aliases/). Add every domain you own, including country sites and blogs on separate domains.

## Example

Of 560 answers, 67 show at least one link to `fernhill.example` or `shop.fernhill.example`. Cited rate is 67 ÷ 560 = **12%**.

## Reading it

- You can be cited without being named, and named without being cited. An answer might quote your brewing guide without recommending you, or recommend you while citing a review site.
- Cited rate only counts sources shown to the reader. Some engines also report pages they read but didn't show. Those are "used" sources, covered in [Sources: URLs and gaps](/docs/sources-urls-and-gaps/).
- To see *which* of your pages get cited and whether that leads to visits, use the [Pages table](/docs/pages-table/).
