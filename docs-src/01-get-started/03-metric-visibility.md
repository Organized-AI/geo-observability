Visibility answers the simplest question: when someone asks, do we come up?

## Definition

**Visibility = answers that name your brand ÷ answers in the current filter.**

An answer "names" your brand when your brand name, one of its aliases, or your optional pattern appears in the answer text. See [Competitors and aliases](/docs/competitors-and-aliases/) for how matching works.

## Example

You track 20 prompts on 4 engines in 1 country, daily, and look at 7 days. That's 20 × 4 × 1 × 7 = 560 answers. If 235 of them name you, visibility is 235 ÷ 560 = **42%**.

## Reading it

- Visibility ignores *where* you appear. Being named last in a long list counts the same as being the first recommendation. Pair it with [position](/docs/metric-position/).
- It's sensitive to your prompt mix. Branded prompts ("Is Fernhill Coffee any good?") almost always name you, so a high branded share inflates visibility. Filter to non-branded prompts to see how often you're found when people don't already know you.
- The change shown next to it compares with the previous period of the same length. Looking at the last 14 days compares with the 14 days before that.
