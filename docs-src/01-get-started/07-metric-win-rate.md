Win rate is a head-to-head measure: when you and a competitor are both named, who comes first?

## Definition

Take the answers that name you **and** at least one tracked competitor. **Win rate is the share of those answers where you're named before every competitor.**

Answers that name only you, or only competitors, are left out. They're covered by [visibility](/docs/metric-visibility/).

## Example

Out of 80 answers that name you and at least one rival, you appear first in 28. Win rate is 28 ÷ 80 = **35%**.

## Reading it

- With one competitor, 50% is an even contest. With four, being first more than 20% of the time is better than an even split.
- Win rate is about order, not tone. Check [sentiment](/docs/metric-sentiment/) and the [chats](/docs/reading-chats/) to confirm being first actually means being recommended.
- On the Overview leaderboard each competitor has its own win rate, worked out the same way from their side.
