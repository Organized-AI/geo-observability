Share of voice tells you how much of the conversation is about you compared with the competitors you track.

## Definition

**Share of voice = your brand's mentions ÷ mentions of all tracked brands (yours plus competitors) in the same answers.**

Only brands you track count. A brand that appears in answers but isn't on your competitor list isn't in the denominator. Brands we spot but you haven't added show up as suggestions on the [competitors](/docs/competitors-and-aliases/) list.

## Example

Across 100 answers, tracked brands are named 300 times in total. Your brand accounts for 60 of those mentions. Share of voice is 60 ÷ 300 = **20%**.

## Reading it

- Adding a competitor lowers everyone's share, including yours, because the denominator grows. If share of voice drops the day you add a rival, that's why.
- Visibility and share of voice can move in opposite directions. If assistants start listing more brands per answer, your visibility can hold steady while your share falls.
- With three tracked brands, an even split is about 33%. Compare against that rather than against 100%.
