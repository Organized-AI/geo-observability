Cards expire and banks decline charges. Here's what happens when that happens to your invoice, and what to do about it.

## Straight after a failed charge

A banner appears at the top of the portal with the date your grace period ends, and owners get an **Update card** button. Everything keeps working during the grace period: daily runs continue, reports update, and you can change settings. Depending on how billing is set up, Stripe may also retry the charge and email the billing contact.

The grace period is 7 days unless your agreement says otherwise.

## When the grace period ends

If the invoice is still unpaid, new runs pause. You can still open every report and export your data, and nothing is deleted. The banner tells you runs are paused.

If Stripe gives up on the invoice, or the subscription ends, the workspace becomes read-only: reports stay open, but changes to prompts, competitors and settings are paused until billing is sorted out. Owners can still open billing to fix it.

## Getting going again

Click **Update card** in the banner, or **Manage billing** on the **Usage & API** page, and add a working card. Once Stripe collects the invoice, the banner clears and runs pick up at the next scheduled pass. If your subscription had already ended, the button reads **Add a card** and starts a new subscription.

Answers that didn't run while runs were paused aren't billed and aren't backfilled, so expect a gap in your trend charts for those days.
