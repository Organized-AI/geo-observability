Topics and tags let you slice every report. Use topics for the main structure and tags for everything else.

## Topics

A topic is a theme, such as "Subscriptions", "Espresso" or "Gifts". Each prompt belongs to one topic.

- We suggest topics during onboarding. Suggested topics have status *suggested* until you keep them.
- Rename a topic any time. Its prompts and history move with it.
- Keep the list short. Five to ten topics is easier to read than thirty.

Every report has a topic filter, and the daily [insights](/docs/overview/) call out moves by topic.

## Tags

Tags are free-form labels, and a prompt can have as many as you like. Use them for anything that cuts across topics:

- funnel stage: `funnel:learn`, `funnel:compare`, `funnel:buy`
- product line: `line:decaf`, `line:equipment`
- campaign: `q4-gifting`
- whether the prompt is branded: `branded`

A `prefix:value` pattern isn't required, but it keeps the tag filter tidy when you have many.

## Personas

There's no separate persona setting. Model personas with two things you already have:

1. **Wording.** Write the prompt the way that person would ask it. "I'm new to espresso and on a budget, what beans should I buy?" gets a different answer from "best espresso beans".
2. **A tag.** Tag those prompts `persona:beginner`, `persona:cafe-owner` and so on, then filter by the tag.

Keep persona detail in the prompt short and natural. Long role-play setups make answers less like what a real person would see.

## Filters combine

Topic, tag, engine, country and date filters all apply together. "Espresso topic, tag `persona:beginner`, Perplexity only, last 30 days" is one click away once your prompts are labelled.
