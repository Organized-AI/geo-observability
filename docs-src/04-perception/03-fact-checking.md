Assistants make factual claims about you: prices, locations, features, founding dates, policies. Fact-checking compares those claims with what you've told us is true.

## How it works

1. You list facts in your [brand profile](/docs/brand-profile/), such as "Free shipping on orders over $40 in the US" or "We ship to the US, Canada and the UK".
2. When an answer names you, the judge pulls out claims it makes about you.
3. Each claim is compared with your facts and given a verdict, such as whether it agrees with them or contradicts them.
4. The Fact-checking view groups claims and counts how many answers made each one.

You can also see each answer's claims when you [read a chat](/docs/reading-chats/).

## Getting good results

- **Write facts as plain sentences.** One fact per line, specific and current.
- **Cover what assistants get wrong.** If you keep seeing a wrong price, add the right price as a fact.
- **Keep facts up to date.** An out-of-date fact makes correct answers look wrong.

A claim that your facts don't cover can't be judged right or wrong. If an important claim keeps coming up, add a fact for it.

## When a claim is wrong

A contradicted claim usually comes from a source the engine read. Open the chats, look at the cited and used sources, and find where the wrong information lives. Fixing it there, or publishing a clear correction on your own site, is what changes future answers. Wrong claims become **fix fact** [actions](/docs/actions/).

## Limits

The judge is a language model. It can misread a claim or miss the link to one of your facts. Treat verdicts as a strong hint, and read the chat before you escalate anything.
