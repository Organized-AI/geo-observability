The brand profile is a short description of your business. We use it to suggest topics and prompts, and to [fact-check](/docs/fact-checking/) what assistants say about you.

## What's in it

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| What you sell | Specialty coffee beans and subscriptions, roasted to order |
| Buyers | Home brewers, from beginners to enthusiasts, and small cafés |
| Markets | US (English), Canada (English, French), UK (English) |
| Facts | One true statement per line |

## Drafting it

During onboarding we read your domain and draft the profile for you. The draft isn't saved until you confirm it. Read it carefully, because a site's homepage often says less than you'd think.

## Writing good facts

Facts are what fact-checking measures against, so make them:

- **Specific.** "Free US shipping on orders over $40" rather than "Affordable shipping".
- **One per line.** Each fact should be checkable on its own.
- **About things assistants discuss.** Prices, plans, where you ship, what you don't do, when you were founded, who you're for.
- **Current.** Update them when things change.

```text
Founded in 2016 in Portland, Oregon.
Subscriptions start at $18 per bag and can be paused any time.
We ship to the US, Canada and the UK.
We don't sell pre-ground coffee.
```

## Markets

Markets pair a country with a language. They guide which countries and languages we suggest prompts for. The countries we actually ask from are set separately in [Engines and countries](/docs/engines-and-countries/).
