A content brief is a short plan for a page, written to answer the questions assistants are being asked and to fill the gaps in what they currently cite.

## Getting a brief

Open an action and ask for a brief. Briefs are generated on request, never automatically. Once generated, the brief is saved with the action, so opening it again shows the same brief.

## What's in a brief

A brief is drawn from the action's evidence: the prompts involved, the answers, what competitors were praised for, the sources that were cited and the claims assistants made. It typically covers:

- who the page is for and the questions it must answer,
- the points that cited pages make and yours doesn't,
- facts from your brand profile that belong on the page,
- a suggested outline.

Briefs are written in Markdown, so you can paste one straight into a doc or a ticket.

## Using it well

- **Treat it as a starting point.** It's generated by a model from the evidence. Check every factual statement against what you know.
- **Write for people first.** Assistants cite pages that answer a question clearly and specifically. A page that reads well to a buyer is the best bet.
- **Say things plainly.** If you want assistants to repeat a fact, such as a price, a policy or who the product is for, state it in a sentence near the top.
- **Close the loop.** When the page is live, mark the action done. The [Pages table](/docs/pages-table/) will show whether crawlers fetch it and whether answers start citing it.
