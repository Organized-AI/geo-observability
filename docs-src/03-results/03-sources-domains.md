Assistants build answers from web pages. The Domains view shows which websites they lean on, and whether those sites mention you.

## Cited and used

Engines touch sources in two ways:

- **Cited**: shown to the reader as a source link.
- **Used**: retrieved by the engine's search but not shown to the reader.

Claude, Gemini and Perplexity report used sources as well as cited ones. ChatGPT reports the sources it shows, so for ChatGPT you'll only see cited sources.

## Domain types

Each domain gets a type:

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Own | Your domains, from your brand settings |
| Competitor | Domains listed on a competitor |
| Editorial | News sites, magazines, review publications |
| Reference | Encyclopedias, dictionaries, documentation |
| UGC | Forums, Q&A sites, community and video platforms |
| Institutional | Government, universities, standards bodies |
| Other | Anything that doesn't fit the above |

Own and competitor come straight from the domains on your brands, so keep those lists complete.

## The columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Citations | How many times the domain was cited |
| Answers | How many answers cited it at least once |
| Share | Its share of all citations in the filter |
| Mentions you | Whether answers citing it also named you |
| Competitors | Which competitors appear in answers citing it |

## What to do with it

Sort by answers and look at the top ten. These sites shape what assistants say about your category. For each, ask: are we on it, and is what it says about us right? The [gaps view](/docs/sources-urls-and-gaps/#gaps) does this check for you.
