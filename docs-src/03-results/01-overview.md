The Overview is the page to open each morning. Everything on it follows the filter bar at the top.

## The filter bar

- **Date range.** Defaults to the last 14 days.
- **Engine, country, topic, tag.** Pick one or several of each.

Every change shown next to a number compares with the **previous period of the same length**, immediately before your range. Looking at 1–14 November compares with 18–31 October.

## Headline numbers

Seven tiles across the top:

| Tile | Short version |
|---|---|
| [Visibility](/docs/metric-visibility/) | Share of answers that name you |
| [Share of voice](/docs/metric-share-of-voice/) | Your share of all tracked brand mentions |
| [Position](/docs/metric-position/) | Average rank when named. Lower is better |
| [Sentiment](/docs/metric-sentiment/) | 0–100, 50 is neutral |
| [Win rate](/docs/metric-win-rate/) | How often you're named before every rival |
| [Cited rate](/docs/metric-cited-rate/) | Share of answers citing your domains |
| Answers | How many answers the numbers are based on |

Check the answer count first. A big swing on 40 answers means much less than the same swing on 4,000.

## Trend and leaderboard

The trend chart shows your daily visibility, share of voice, position and sentiment next to your competitors. The leaderboard ranks every tracked brand on the same metrics for the whole range.

## By engine

Visibility split by engine, with the answer count for each. It's common to do well on one engine and poorly on another, because each one searches and cites differently.

## Top sources and recent chats

The domains cited most often in your filtered answers, and the latest answers with whether you were named and at what rank. Click through to [Sources](/docs/sources-domains/) or a [chat](/docs/reading-chats/).

## Insights

Every day at 09:00 UTC we compare each project with the previous period and write three to five plain sentences about what changed. They cover:

- visibility moves by engine and by topic,
- prompts you started or stopped appearing in,
- new domains citing your rivals but not you,
- cited pages that AI crawlers stopped fetching,
- shifts in AI referrals and conversions.

Each insight links to the evidence behind it, so you can check it rather than take it on trust.

## Actions

The open [actions](/docs/actions/) with the highest expected impact, so the Overview ends with something to do.
