The URLs view goes one level below domains, to the exact pages assistants cite. The gaps view turns that into a to-do list.

## URLs

Pick a domain, or look across all of them. Each row is one page:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Title | The page title |
| Citations | Times cited |
| Answers | Answers that cited or used it |
| Cited | Times shown to the reader |
| Used | Times retrieved but not shown |
| First seen / last seen | The first and latest day it appeared |

A page with many *used* hits but few *cited* hits is being read but not credited. That's common for pages that hold the facts but not a quotable summary. "Used" counts come from Claude, Gemini and Perplexity only.

For your own pages, the [Pages table](/docs/pages-table/) adds crawler visits, live fetches, referrals and conversions.

## Gaps

The gaps view lists **domains that appear in answers naming a competitor but not you.** These are the sites feeding your rivals' visibility.

Work through it from the top:

1. Open the domain and read the pages that were cited.
2. If it's a list or review site, see whether you can be included. These become **get listed** [actions](/docs/actions/).
3. If it's a forum or Q&A site, see what people say and whether there's a real question you can answer honestly.
4. If it's a competitor's own site, read what they publish that you don't. That might become a **new page** action.

Gaps are worked out over your current filter, so narrow it to one topic or engine for a sharper list.
